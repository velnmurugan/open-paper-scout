# Scouts in the wild

One row per reader's scout. Make the row with `python evaluate.py row` in
your own copy, after labeling at least 15 papers, and add it here in a pull
request (see CONTRIBUTING.md). Numbers are on each person's own labels, so
compare them with care: a 0.9 on an easy interest isn't better than a 0.7
on a hard one.
